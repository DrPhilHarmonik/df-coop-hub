Keys

Keys follow Steam DF's defaults.
WASD, arrows or drag: move (Shift faster)
e / c (or < >, wheel): up / down a level
[ ] or Ctrl+wheel: zoom
Space pause · Esc back / cancel
Click a player to jump to their view
Click a creature to inspect it; click an announcement to go there
m dig, then u channel, t stairs (again: down, up), r ramp, m dig; x erase
In the dig menu, p / P: priority up / down (1 is done first)
Click two corners, or Enter at each corner
b build, with DF's menu letters: bofp farm plot, bpr door
p stockpiles · z zones · o work orders · q squads · n nobles · u citizens · l work details · k stocks · N announcements
X remove a building · g ping · Enter chat (in Look)
Right-click: cancel
Drag to pan, pinch to zoom
Tap a tile to point at it
▲ ▼ change level
